PACKING GUIDE

Make every activation easy

Use these same instructions in the printed activation pack and on the website.

01

iPhone

Unlock the iPhone and hold its top edge against the Cardence card. Keep it still for a moment. If tapping does not work, scan the QR code with the Camera app.

02

Android

Turn NFC on in Settings, unlock the phone and hold its upper back against the Cardence card. Antenna position varies, so move slowly until the phone reacts.
