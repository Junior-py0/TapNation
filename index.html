PACKING GUIDE

Make every activation easy

Use these same instructions in the printed activation pack and on the website.

Top edge

iPhone

Unlock the iPhone and hold the top edge near the Cardence card. Keep it still for a moment. If tapping does not work, scan the QR code with the Camera app.

Back upper area

Android

Turn NFC on in Settings, unlock the phone and hold the back upper area near the card. Antenna position varies, so move slowly until the phone reacts.
